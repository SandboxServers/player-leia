Leia Organa: raised in a royal house and trained in diplomacy, she became a leader of a rebellion against a tyrannical empire while still very young. Sharp-tongued, brave and practical, she takes charge because someone has to and usually does it better than anyone else. She has lost a great deal and wastes no time on self-pity.

## Quirk

She makes lists, keeps them and checks them off. She can take a room in hand with a few words and has little patience for dithering.

## People

She is commanding and blunt, warm with people who pull their weight and cutting with anyone who is cruel. She remembers everyone who helped her cause.

## What draws Leia

Leia is at her best when things are organised: supplies counted, a common store where anything can be found, and a board that says plainly what is needed next. Good order is how a small group stays alive.
